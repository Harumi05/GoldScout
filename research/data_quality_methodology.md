# Frozen historical evidence — schema v1

The versioned snapshot is [`data_quality_manifest.json`](data_quality_manifest.json). Regenerate a machine-local copy with `python -m research.build_data_quality_manifest`; the default output is `research/output/data_quality_manifest.json`, which remains Git-ignored. The builder reads `processed_inputs.json` and the four already persisted bar files, verifies their recorded byte sizes, and **does not reopen the 132M-tick CSV**. A changed source file requires the existing replay/rebuild workflow, not an automatic reinterpretation of this frozen snapshot. Source paths are deliberately omitted from the committed JSON.

`schema_version=1` identifies the field contract. `dataset_id` is derived from the recorded SHA-256 fingerprint. `source_files` records filename, byte size, mtime, fingerprint and adoption status; the full local path remains only in the ignored processed-input manifest. The adopted manifest knows `ticks_valid`, but its `ticks_read`, `ticks_discarded` and `duplicates` are null. Null means **unknown**, not zero. Existing M1/M15/H1/H4 counts come from their persisted JSONL files; derived M5/D1 counts are capability estimates from closed M1 and are **not materialized datasets**.

For consecutive persisted M1 starts, a gap is the elapsed start-to-start interval exceeding 1 minute, 1 hour or 1 day respectively. The categories overlap. `maximum_elapsed_minutes` includes the endpoints' full start-to-start interval, not merely missing minutes. No empty bar is inserted. `UNKNOWN_REASON` does not prove market closure, data corruption, or a broker pause.

Spread median/p95/p99 are **unweighted across observed M1 bars** of each bar's `avg_spread`, in XAUUSD price units. Percentiles sort values and linearly interpolate at `(n-1)*p`. `maximum_observed_tick_spread_from_m1` is the maximum persisted M1 `max_spread`, not a reconstruction of the tick path. M1 `tick_count` median/p95 use the same percentile rule; low activity means `tick_count <= 5`. Quote ticks are not guaranteed executed volume.

M5 capability uses five aligned server-wall-clock minutes. D1 capability uses an explicit `broker_day_boundary=00:00` **only to inventory technical date buckets**; it is not a verified broker rollover or UTC midnight. A bucket emits only when a later closed M1 proves the boundary was crossed; the final bucket stays unclosed. `complete` requires all expected 5 or 1,440 minutes and is a coverage property, **not a data-quality verdict**: normal pauses and short Sunday sessions can be sparse. `available_at` is not earlier than the first later closed M1. See [`multi_strategy_causal_contract.md`](multi_strategy_causal_contract.md).

Broker-server UTC offset and rollover have not been verified. Therefore `timezone.status=UNKNOWN`, `broker_server_utc_offset=null`, `session_attribution_allowed=false`, and `d1_session_status=UNVERIFIED`. Do not infer them from the PC clock or relabel technical D1 buckets as sessions. Reproducibility also depends on the recorded replay output version and unchanged persisted bars; byte-size verification is a guard, not a cryptographic hash of each bar file.
